yamaha YZF‑R3

The Yamaha YZF‑R3 is a lightweight sport motorcycle designed for both beginner and experienced riders. It belongs to Yamaha’s “R-Series,” known for sporty, aerodynamic styling inspired by MotoGP bikes. The R3 is powered by a 321 cc, liquid-cooled, parallel-twin engine with a 6-speed manual transmission, delivering a balance of manageable power and high-rev performance. Its chassis is lightweight and nimble, featuring an inverted front fork, Monocross rear shock, and disc brakes with ABS, making it well-suited for both city riding and light track use. Recent models include modern features such as an LCD instrument panel, LED lighting, smartphone connectivity via Yamaha Y-Connect, and an assist & slipper clutch for smoother shifting. With its combination of sporty design, efficient engine, and user-friendly handling, the YZF‑R3 is popular among riders seeking a fun, agile, and stylish supersport motorcycle.

Yamaha MT‑07

The Yamaha MT‑07 is a middleweight “naked” / standard motorcycle, known for its great balance of performance, agility, and usability. It’s powered by a 689 cc liquid-cooled, 4-stroke, parallel-twin (inline‑2) “CP2” engine.
